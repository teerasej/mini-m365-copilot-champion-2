WORKSHOP DOCUMENT CHECKLIST | SYNTHETIC TRAINING MATERIAL
This is a fictional checklist for the class. It does not represent Krungsri policy.

D01 | Application intake note | Case ID, business name, request purpose and customer-reported payment terms.
D02 | Business profile | Business name and business activity. Compare the name against D01.
D03 | Cash flow summary | Case ID and reporting period. An inventory entry must identify the actual document received.
D04 | Receivables summary | Case ID and payment terms. Compare the terms against D01. Record a payment terms conflict on D04 and cite both records.

Status vocabulary
- Received: listed in the case inventory and consistent with the fields checked here.
- Missing: required item is absent or explicitly not received.
- Discrepancy: item is present but the case evidence conflicts. Record both values and their references.
- Needs verification: item or required detail cannot be confirmed from the supplied records.

Workshop routing
- Ready for staff review: D01–D04 are Received. Proposed owner: Credit Operations Reviewer.
- Follow-up required: at least one Missing, Discrepancy, or Needs verification item. Proposed owner: Relationship Manager, who confirms or collects the evidence before handoff.
- Credit decision request: refer the request to the authorized Credit Approver. Document completeness is separate from lending decisions.
